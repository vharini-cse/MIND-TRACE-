THE IDEA

One situation can create an entire chain of responses.

Something happens. A thought appears. An emotion follows. Then we respond.

MindTrace gives you a moment to slow that process down and look at each part separately.

EXPLORE

Common thinking patterns.

Learning about patterns can help us notice how our interpretations influence our responses.

01
◌

Mind Reading

Assuming you know what another person is thinking or feeling without having enough information.

EVERYDAY EXAMPLE

“They didn't reply, so they must be upset with me.”

02
◇

Jumping to Conclusions

Reaching a conclusion before you have enough information about what happened.

EVERYDAY EXAMPLE

“The meeting was quiet, so everyone must dislike my idea.”

03
△

Negative Prediction

Expecting a difficult outcome before there is enough evidence to know what will happen.

EVERYDAY EXAMPLE

“I'm definitely going to mess up the presentation.”

04
↔

All-or-Nothing Thinking

Viewing experiences mainly as complete success or complete failure rather than on a spectrum.

EVERYDAY EXAMPLE

“If I don't do this perfectly, I've failed.”

05
↻

Overgeneralization

Taking one experience and applying it broadly to future situations.

EVERYDAY EXAMPLE

“That interview went badly. I am always bad at interviews.”

06
◎

Personalization

Interpreting an event as being mainly about yourself when other explanations may exist.

EVERYDAY EXAMPLE

“They seem quiet today. I must have done something wrong.”
